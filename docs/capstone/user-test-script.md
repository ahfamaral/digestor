# Digestor: 20-minute user test (short script)

A short version of the full protocol in [user-test.md](user-test.md), for one tester when there is no time for the 40-minute session. It focuses on what the brief's Case 2 asks: **the agent's accuracy and responsiveness, judged by a user**. Print this page and the notes sheet below; fill them in by hand, then copy the results into the results template of `user-test.md`.

## Before the tester arrives (5 min, not counted)

- [ ] https://digestor.freitasdoamaral.com open in a fresh browser window, logged in. The password is given in person, never written down.
- [ ] The Osang × Merkel interview shows as ready on Sessions. Undo any approvals from an earlier test on its Briefing page.
- [ ] Phone stopwatch ready. Screen recording ready (only if the tester agrees).
- [ ] This page and the notes sheet printed, a pen.

## Consent (read aloud, 1 min)

"We are testing the software, not you. With your agreement I will record the screen and your voice. My notes use a number, not your name. They are used only for this project, and you can stop at any time or ask me to delete them."

Agrees: yes / no · Recording: yes / no

## Start (read aloud)

"You are preparing a short piece about this interview. Please think aloud: say what you look at, what you expect and what confuses you. I can't help, but I will take notes. There are no wrong answers."

Read each task aloud. Do not say the words in brackets. If the tester is stuck for 3 minutes, say "Let's move on" and mark the task as not done.

## Tasks (about 15 min)

**T1. Find and keep (4 min).** "Find where Angela Merkel talks about Putin and mark the parts you would use."
[Briefing page, a topic card, Approve on a moment.] Done when one moment about Putin is Approved.

**T2. Ask and check (5 min).** "Ask the tool a question you would want answered before publishing. Then decide whether you believe the answer."
[Ask button or `/`. Time the answer: from pressing Enter to the full answer. The tester should open the video at a cited time.]
Done when the tester checks one cited time against the video and says whether it matches.

**T3. Let the agent change something (4 min).** "Ask the tool to make a clip for you, for example the best 30 seconds about Putin. Look at what it proposes before anything happens."
[Approval card: Approve, Edit or Reject. Time the card: from Enter to the card.]
Done when the tester says what will change and picks one of the three on purpose.

**T4. Get it out (2 min).** "Get a clip out of the tool so you could send it to an editor."
[Export, Download all or a single clip.] Done when a file is downloaded and the tester says what is in it.

## After the tasks (4 min)

Ask aloud and write the answers word for word:

1. "How much would you trust the answers you got? Why?" (1 = not at all, 5 = completely, and the reason)
2. "Was it quick enough to use while working on a story?" (1 = far too slow, 5 = quick enough, and the reason)
3. "What was the most difficult moment?"
4. "What did the tool do that you didn't expect?"

## Reporting it

Copy the numbers into `user-test.md` (results template) and add one paragraph to `docs/capstone/evaluation.md` under a "User test" heading: who tested (role, not name), the four task results, the two Case 2 numbers (answer time, cited times that matched) and the trust and speed scores with the tester's reasons. With one tester this is an observation, not a statistic; say so.
